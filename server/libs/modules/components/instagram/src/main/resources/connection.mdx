## Connection Setup

This component uses the Instagram API with Instagram Login. It works only with Instagram professional accounts (business or creator). Personal accounts cannot publish through the API.

### Switch your Instagram account to a professional account

1. In the Instagram app, open your profile and go to **Settings and activity**.
2. Open **Account type and tools** and choose **Switch to professional account**.
3. Pick **Business** or **Creator** and finish the steps.

### Create a Meta app

1. Go to [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/apps) and click **Create App**.
2. Choose the **Business** app type, or the use case **Manage messaging and content on Instagram**, and finish the steps.
3. In the app dashboard, add the **Instagram** product and open **API setup with Instagram login**.
4. Under **Set up Instagram business login**, add the ByteChef OAuth redirect URL as a valid redirect URL, for example `https://app.bytechef.io/callback`. For a self-hosted instance use `https://<your ByteChef host>/callback`.
5. Copy the **Instagram app ID** and the **Instagram app secret**. Do not use the Facebook app ID and secret shown on the basic settings page.
6. While the app is in development mode, add your Instagram account as an **Instagram Tester** under **App roles**, then accept the invitation in the Instagram app or on the Instagram website under **Apps and websites**.

### Create the connection in ByteChef

1. Paste the Instagram app ID into **Instagram App ID** and the Instagram app secret into **Instagram App Secret**.
2. Keep the default scopes:
    - `instagram_business_basic` to read the account and its media.
    - `instagram_business_content_publish` to publish images, carousels and reels.
    - `instagram_business_manage_comments` to read comments and reply to them.
3. Click **Connect** and log in with your Instagram professional account.

### Tokens

ByteChef exchanges the short-lived token from the login for a long-lived token that is valid for 60 days. Instagram does not issue a refresh token. To keep the connection working, reconnect it before the 60 days run out. Meta describes how long-lived tokens can be refreshed in [its documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/business-login#refresh-a-long-lived-token).

### App review

To use the connection with Instagram accounts that are not testers of your app, submit the app for App Review and request advanced access for the three permissions above.
